Key Findings

What the analysis indicates

Strongest overall cases

Solar PV, Offshore Wind and Battery Storage demonstrate the strongest overall investment cases under the analysis.

High potential, practical constraints

Heat Pumps show high technological attractiveness, but deployment and workforce constraints remain important.

Selective long-term opportunity

Hydrogen shows significant long-term decarbonisation potential, but supports a more selective investment approach.

Weakest financial case

CCUS currently presents the weakest financial case under the assumptions used in the model.

Portfolio-level conclusion

Investment decisions are stronger when financial return, risk, technology maturity and decarbonisation impact are considered together.
